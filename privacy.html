Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what Estes Family Regenerative Farm, LLC collects in connection with a trailer rental, why, how long it's kept, and who it's shared with. This website itself does not collect payment or identity information — that happens through our booking partner (Lockii) and payment/identity processor (Stripe) once you begin a booking.

What we collect

  • Identity verification: a photo of your driver's license and a selfie, collected and verified by Stripe Identity, including your name and date of birth as confirmed on the license.
  • Booking & contact information: name, phone number, email, and vehicle information, collected by Lockii at booking.
  • GPS location data: the trailer carries a GPS tracker that reports its location while rented, for security and recovery purposes.
  • Camera footage: a WiFi camera covers the pickup/return area at the shelter and records pickup and return activity.

Why we collect it

Identity verification confirms you are who you say you are before a pickup code is issued — this is a software-enforced step, required for every rental, not a discretionary check. GPS and camera data protect against theft and support insurance and dispute resolution if a trailer is damaged, lost, or not returned. Contact information is used to coordinate your rental and reach you if needed.

How long we keep it

Identity verification and booking records are retained for as long as needed to support the rental relationship, resolve any disputes, and meet insurance and tax record-keeping requirements, after which they are deleted or retained only in de-identified form. Stripe and Lockii each also retain data according to their own policies for the portions of the process they handle directly.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run the rental: Stripe (payment processing and identity verification) and Lockii (booking and access management). We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Payment security

We never see or store your card number. All payment card data is handled directly by Stripe.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you or request it be deleted, subject to what we're required to retain for legal, tax, or insurance purposes. Contact us at the address below.
