Our Story

"To love and heal the earth — our little part of it — by serving and worshiping its Creator, by honoring its residents, and by blessing those who feast from its gifts."

Estes Family Regenerative Farm started in 2022 as a family effort to grow really good food — food that's local, chemical-free, nutrient-dense, and grown with real care for the land it comes from.

We believe healthy soil grows healthy food, so we farm using natural, regenerative practices: building organic matter in the ground, protecting soil life by minimizing tillage, capturing rainwater on-site, and encouraging biodiversity wherever we can.

We have big dreams for what this farm can become — but we're starting modestly, on purpose. Today, that means pasture-raised eggs year-round and seasonal meat birds, raised with care and processed through a licensed USDA facility. As the farm grows, so will what we're able to offer.

The trailer rental is a newer, smaller part of the story — a practical way to put idle equipment to good use for neighbors, while the farm itself keeps growing toward what it's meant to be.

Thank you for supporting a small, family farm doing things the slow way, on purpose.
